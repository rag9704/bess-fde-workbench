## Where the gap comes from

- Forecast error (gradient-boosting MPC vs perfect-forecast MPC): **¥95,795**
- Rolling 24 h horizon and terminal reserve (perfect-forecast MPC vs hindsight): **¥44,415**
- Saved by the real-time peak guard (gradient-boosting MPC without vs with the guard): **¥446,406**
- Saved by using the published JEPX day-ahead prices instead of forecasting them: **¥140,507**

Gradient boosting per target (chosen on the last 336 h of training, validation MAE):
- `load_kw`: gbm (gbm 24.86 vs persistence 38.39)
- `solar_kw`: gbm (gbm 49.40 vs persistence 51.54)
- `tokyo_price_yen_kwh`: gbm (gbm 4.32 vs persistence 4.53)
